Questions

Straight answers.

Is Amaze health insurance?

No. Amaze is a virtual care membership — a subscription, in their own words. It doesn't replace a medical plan and it won't pay a hospital bill. It sits alongside your coverage and handles the everyday things.

Who does the membership cover?

You, your spouse or partner, and your children up to age 26 — all on one flat price. You add them inside the app once you're enrolled.

Is there a charge each time we use it?

No. There's no fee per visit and no appointment needed, however many times you use it in a month.

Can I cancel?

Yes. It renews month to month and you can cancel at any time. Your account stays with you afterwards, with the free features — the search tool, symptom checker and video library.
