What else the new contract changed

Retirement

Rebuilding what AIPP was for

For many agents, AIPP worked as the retirement plan. Payments now run through 2028 and then depend on sales targets. We can show you tax-advantaged strategies you own and control, including indexed universal life designed for retirement income.

Coverage nobody can take away

You've told clients for years not to rely only on coverage through work. NASFA's plan includes no life insurance. Individually owned coverage stays with you whatever happens to your contract.
